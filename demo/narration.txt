Meet pmptr — a minimal virtual teleprompter that floats over whatever you do on screen.

This is the control window. Paste your script here… or load a sample to get started in one click.

Tune reading speed, text size, and line height until the pace feels natural.

Switch to Look to pick colors, opacity, and mirror mode — great for camera setups.

Now open the floating prompter. Transparent. Frameless. Always on top.

Hit Space to play. Your words scroll smoothly at the speed you chose.

Press L to lock click-through — your mouse keeps working on whatever is underneath.

Need a quick change? Nudge the speed live. Reset with R. Pause with Space.

pmptr. Cue your words. Keep the stage clear.
Free and open source — install with npm, or download for Mac, Windows, and Linux.
